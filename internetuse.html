  • Tech Usage
People use the internet everywhere, all the time, and using it safely and responsibly is very important in the modern day. While it’s easy to waste hours scrolling on a phone without noticing, It’s important to catch yourself to take breaks and limit the overall time you spend on screens. It’s also important to try and balance being online with stuff in real life, like going outside, or talking to friends in person, or even doing something productive. Another important thing to manage online is your safety. You have to be careful with what you post. People online can often be kinda crazy to say the least, and people really don’t need to know everything about your life anyways. The internet is a fun and useful tool for the world, and in the current day and age it’s used for so much but you should understand that being on it 24/7 is very unhealthy.
